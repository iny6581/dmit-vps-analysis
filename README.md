## DMIT's Network Tiers Explained

Before diving into the plan table, it helps to understand DMIT's three-tier structure. Every datacenter offers the same tiers, but the routing differs dramatically:

**Premium (CN2 GIA)**
The flagship. All three major Chinese carriers (China Telecom, Unicom, Mobile) routed via CN2 GIA on both inbound and outbound. Lowest latency to Chinese networks, minimal packet loss even during peak hours. Price accordingly.

**Eyeball (CMIN2 / CMI)**
Mid-tier. Uses CMIN2 or CMI routing — not quite CN2 GIA, but meaningfully better than commodity international routing. Good price-to-performance balance for users who need some China optimization without premium pricing.

**Tier 1 (International)**
No China optimization. Standard international BGP routing with major carriers. Fastest bandwidth, lowest price. Works great for non-China workloads — US content, European APIs, Singapore-local projects.
